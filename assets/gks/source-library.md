### Application Method

#### Degree programs

Applicants must submit their application either to the Korean Embassy in their home country or to one of the GKS-designated universities.

 Embassy Track: Online application through the Study in Korea website (www.studyinkorea.go.kr)
 University Track: Submit the application directly to a GKS-designated university [...] #### General Program

Covering various fields, such as humanities, social studies, natural sciences, and arts and physical education, etc.

#### Specialization (UIC)

Science and engineering departments focused on customized curriculum and field experience through industry-university collaboration   
 \ Available only for associate and bachelor's degree programs
