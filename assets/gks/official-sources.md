## Stable official overview

GKS is a Korean government scholarship for international students. Depending on the program, support can include Korean-language training, degree study, tuition, airfare, medical insurance, and allowances. Benefits, amounts, quotas, dates, and program names are year-specific.

Applicants normally use one route: **Embassy Track**, through the Korean diplomatic mission in the applicant's country of citizenship, or **University Track**, directly through a designated university. Track rules, available universities, submission method, and document requirements must be read from the current year's guideline and local notice.

The general published undergraduate criteria include non-Korean citizenship for the applicant and both parents, the applicable age limit, the required preceding education, and the current academic-performance threshold. The general published graduate criteria include non-Korean citizenship for the applicant and both parents, the applicable age limit, the required preceding degree, and the current academic-performance threshold. These are screening baselines, not a selection guarantee.

Common application materials can include the application form, personal statement, study plan, recommendation letter(s), graduation or expected-graduation proof, official transcripts, citizenship/family-relation proof, and supporting test or award documents where applicable. The current guideline decides which are mandatory, how many copies are needed, translation rules, certification/apostille requirements, and the submission deadline.

Embassies and universities conduct the first screening for their respective tracks. Applicants must follow the receiving embassy's or university's additional instructions. A university list, quota, deadline, or country-specific process in an older year must not be presented as current.

## Safe answer rules

- Cite the relevant year and official source URL whenever a fact is year-specific.
- If the question asks for a current deadline, quota, university, embassy instruction, or required document that is not stated in this offline context, say that the current notice must be checked.
- Do not infer Cameroon-specific embassy procedures from another country. For a Cameroon applicant, advise checking the current Korean Embassy in Cameroon notice in addition to NIIED guidance.
- Never claim that a scholarship, admission, visa, or document outcome is guaranteed.
